## About Second Chance Paroles

Second Chance Paroles is a feature within the A New Me platform focused on the transition from incarceration to community life. It brings together essential information and practical support intended to help parolees navigate reentry and identify useful community resources.

The platform is designed for individuals rebuilding their lives under community supervision, as well as people and organizations looking for structured reentry guidance. By making relevant resources easier to access, Second Chance Paroles addresses common barriers that can complicate a successful return to the community.
